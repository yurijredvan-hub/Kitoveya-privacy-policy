Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-30

1. General Information

Kitoveya helps users organize medicine inventory and first aid kits, including medicine quantities, strength or concentration shown on packaging, expiration dates, and local stock and expiry alerts.

The application is intended for informational and organizational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

2. Data Storage

All information entered into the application is stored locally on the user's device.

Examples of stored information may include:

  • medicine names;
  • quantities;
  • medicine strength or concentration shown on packaging;
  • expiration dates;
  • categories;
  • medicine forms;
  • first aid kit information;
  • application settings;
  • notification preferences.

3. Data Transmission

The application does not transmit personal or medical information to external servers.

The application does not automatically collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share user-entered medicine data with third parties. Files shared or exported at the user's request are described in the Data Backup section.

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

At the time of publication, the application does not use analytics systems or crash-reporting services.

If analytics or diagnostics are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Advertising

Future versions of the application may display advertisements.

Advertising providers may collect technical information required for ad delivery in accordance with their own privacy policies.

If advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect the relevant data practices.

6. Notifications

The application may use local device notifications for medicine expiration dates and low stock levels configured by the user. These are inventory alerts only.

Notification scheduling is performed locally on the device.

7. Permissions

The application may request permissions necessary for its functionality, including notification permissions.

Permissions are used only for the intended features and can be revoked by the user through device settings.

8. Data Backup

Current versions store data locally on the device.

Users can export and restore local backup files containing only medicine inventory, first aid kits, categories, medicine forms, and shopping lists. Application preferences and notification settings are not included. New backups do not include medicine intake records from earlier versions. When an older backup is restored, only inventory data is imported; medicine intake data is ignored. Exported files are not encrypted by the application. Users choose where to save or share them and are responsible for protecting them; a chosen storage or sharing provider may process the file under its own policies. The application does not automatically upload these files or synchronize them with a server. Operating-system backup or device-transfer services may separately copy application data according to the user's device and account settings and the service provider's policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws.

Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising application usage by minors.

10. Medical Disclaimer

The application does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or pharmaceutical consultation.

Users should always consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical decisions.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Updated versions will be published within the application.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, users may contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's official store page.

13. Notification reliability

Local stock and expiry notifications may arrive late, be duplicated, or not arrive because of device settings, disabled permissions, battery saving, operating-system restrictions, restarts, or software faults. They are supplementary inventory aids, not a medical monitoring or emergency service. Do not rely on them as the sole means of checking medicine quantities or expiration dates; check the inventory and product packaging independently. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer is not liable for consequences of delayed, missed, or incorrect notifications; this does not exclude liability or consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

14. Inventory and records from earlier versions

The application manages inventory only. Medicine strength or concentration describes the medicine as shown on its packaging; it is not a prescribed dose or instructions for taking medicine. The application has no prescribed dosing or medicine intake features. It does not measure adherence or track treatment. Anyone with access to an unlocked device or an exported backup may see the inventory and other user-entered information.

After an upgrade from an earlier version, old medicine intake records may remain stored locally on the device. The current application does not use these records or make them available, and new backup exports do not include them. Clearing all data in the application, restoring a backup, wiping the application's device data, or uninstalling the application removes these local records. Previously exported files and copies held by operating-system backup, device-transfer, or storage services are separate and must be managed through the relevant service.
