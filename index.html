2. Information stored on your device

Kitoveya stores information you enter in its local application storage: medicine and supply names; quantities and minimum stock levels; strength or concentration printed on packaging; expiry dates; kits, categories and forms; notes; and shopping lists. Notes or inventory may contain personal or sensitive information if you enter it.

The application also stores preferences such as language, theme, field hints and notification settings, the accepted legal-document revision, and local state needed to manage inventory alerts. The operating system may hold scheduled alerts and display their contents in notifications.

No Kitoveya account, name, email address or phone number is required to use the application. It does not request access to contacts, location, camera, microphone, health platforms or your inventory of installed applications.

3. How information is used

Local information is used to display and organize inventory, calculate stock and expiry summaries, manage shopping lists, create or restore requested backups, apply preferences, and deliver configured stock and expiry alerts.

Kitoveya does not provide prescribed dosing, intake schedules, intake reminders, adherence monitoring or treatment tracking. Packaging strength describes a product; it is not an instruction for taking medicine.

4. Transmission and sharing

The developer does not receive your inventory, preferences or notification contents through the application. The current application does not automatically upload or synchronize them, sell or rent them, or send them to advertising, analytics or crash-reporting services.

The exceptions initiated by you are exporting or sharing a backup, opening an external service, and contacting support, as described below. The operating system may separately back up or transfer application data according to your device and account settings. This is not Kitoveya cloud synchronization.

5. Advertising, analytics and purchases

The current application contains no advertisements, advertising SDK, analytics service, developer-operated crash-reporting service, paid subscription or in-app purchase. It does not read the advertising identifier for these purposes.

If such features are added, their actual data practices and any required choices or permissions will be disclosed before they are used. This policy does not authorize hypothetical future data collection.

6. Stock and expiry notifications

Stock and expiry notifications are generated locally using your inventory and settings. Notification contents may reveal product names or stock information to anyone who can see the device or its lock screen. You can disable alerts in Kitoveya or manage notification permission and lock-screen visibility in device settings.

Notification permission allows displaying alerts; exact-alarm access supports the selected inventory-alert times; boot and time-change handling restores or adjusts local scheduling. These capabilities are not used for medication intake reminders or tracking you.

7. Backups and selected files

Export creates an unencrypted JSON file containing inventory, kits, categories, forms and shopping lists. Current exports exclude application preferences, notification settings and retired intake records. Restoring a selected backup replaces the current inventory; intake data in older backup formats is ignored.

The system file picker and share sheet let you choose a file, destination or recipient. A receiving application, storage provider or person may read and process an exported file under its own rules. Kitoveya does not automatically connect to your Google account or Google Drive. Temporary export copies may remain in application cache until that cache is cleared by you or the system.

8. Retention and deletion

Current inventory is retained locally until you change or delete records, clear inventory, replace it by restoring a backup, or remove the application's device data. Kit settings include the option to clear inventory data. It resets the inventory database, including retired intake records, but does not reset all preferences or the legal-acceptance setting.

To remove all local application data and cached export files, use Android Settings, open Kitoveya's application storage and select Clear storage/Clear data, or uninstall the application. Labels vary by device. Preferences otherwise remain until changed or application data is cleared.

Deleting local data does not delete files you previously exported, files sent to recipients, or copies held by operating-system backup or transfer services. Delete these separately through the relevant location or provider. A system restore may restore previously backed-up data. There is no Kitoveya server account or server inventory for the developer to delete remotely.

9. Security

Data is stored in the application's operating-system-managed storage. Kitoveya does not add its own database encryption or an additional application password. Device security, backups and notification visibility depend on your device settings. Use a device lock, protect exported files and avoid entering information you do not want visible to someone with access to your device or files. No storage or software can be guaranteed completely secure.
