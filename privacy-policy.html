Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

1. General Information

Kitoveya is designed to help users manage information about medicines, first aid kits, medicine quantities, expiration dates, and related notifications.

The application is intended for informational and organizational purposes only and does not provide medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment recommendations.

2. Data Storage

All information entered into the application is stored locally on the user's device.

Examples of stored information may include:

  • medicine names;
  • quantities;
  • expiration dates;
  • categories;
  • medicine forms;
  • first aid kit information;
  • application settings;
  • notification preferences.

3. Data Transmission

The application does not transmit personal or medical information to external servers.

The application does not automatically collect, transmit, sell, rent, or share user-entered medicine data with third parties. Files shared or exported at the user's request are described in the Data Backup section.

4. Analytics and Diagnostics

At the time of publication, the application does not use analytics systems or crash-reporting services.

If analytics or diagnostics are introduced in future versions, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

5. Advertising

Future versions of the application may display advertisements.

Advertising providers may collect technical information required for ad delivery in accordance with their own privacy policies.

If advertising is introduced, this Privacy Policy may be updated to reflect the relevant data practices.

6. Notifications

The application may use local device notifications to remind users about:

  • medicine expiration dates;
  • low stock levels;
  • other medicine-related events configured by the user.

Notification scheduling is performed locally on the device.

7. Permissions

The application may request permissions necessary for its functionality, including notification permissions.

Permissions are used only for the intended features and can be revoked by the user through device settings.

8. Data Backup

Current versions store data locally on the device.

Users can export and restore local backup files containing their inventory, intake plans, groups, and intake history. Exported files are not encrypted by the application. Users choose where to save or share them and are responsible for protecting them; a chosen storage or sharing provider may process the file under its own policies. The application does not automatically upload these files or synchronize them with a server. Operating-system backup or device-transfer services may separately copy application data according to the user's device and account settings and the service provider's policies.

9. Children's Privacy

The application is not specifically directed toward children under the age required by applicable laws.

Parents or guardians are responsible for supervising application usage by minors.

10. Medical Disclaimer

The application does not replace professional medical advice, diagnosis, treatment, or pharmaceutical consultation.

Users should always consult qualified healthcare professionals regarding medical decisions.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time.

Updated versions will be published within the application.

12. Contact

For questions regarding this Privacy Policy, users may contact the developer using the contact information provided on the application's official store page.

13. Notification reliability

Notifications and reminders may arrive late, be duplicated, or not arrive because of device settings, disabled permissions, battery saving, operating-system restrictions, restarts, or software faults. They are supplementary organizational aids, not a medical monitoring or emergency service. Do not rely on them as the sole means of checking medicine expiry or following a dosing schedule prescribed by a healthcare professional; use an independent reminder or check when timing matters. To the extent permitted by applicable law, the developer is not liable for consequences of delayed, missed, or incorrect notifications; this does not exclude liability or consumer rights that cannot lawfully be excluded.

14. Intake records and reminders

User-entered doses, units, course dates, times, weekdays, reminder settings, groups, intake marks, and notes are stored locally. Intake history retains medicine names and dose information when a medicine or first aid kit is deleted; deleting inventory stops its courses but does not erase history. Removing an archived course keeps its history. Users can delete individual history entries or clear all visible intake history. Separate local stock-deduction bookkeeping is retained to prevent the same intake from deducting stock twice after history is cleared; it does not retain deleted history notes. Clearing all application data or restoring a backup replaces the current intake records and stock bookkeeping. Local intake notifications use a general reminder message rather than medicine names or doses, but anyone with access to an unlocked device or an exported backup may see sensitive information.
